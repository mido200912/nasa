# VULCAN-X product context

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, habitat engineers, students, and space-science readers exploring an educational combustion demonstrator.

## Product Purpose

Explore space-habitat scenarios, source-backed catalog metadata, and explicitly unvalidated AI explanations. This is not an operational fire-safety system or a NASA-endorsed predictive model.

## Brand Personality

Technical, austere, precise. Preserve the existing aerospace workbench described in `vulcan-x/Frontend/design.md`.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling, decorative glass panels, fabricated scientific certainty, and presenting illustrative presets as measured conditions.

## Design Principles

- Keep experimental AI explanations, offline illustrations, and approved scientific workflows distinct.
- Describe the request, its persistence, provenance, failures, and limitations honestly.
- Require explicit user inputs; never silently promote offline presets to scientific evidence.
- Reuse the dashboard's existing controls and visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, associated labels, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion support, and layouts that fit narrow viewports and enlarged text.
