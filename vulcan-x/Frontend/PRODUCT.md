# VULCAN-X

## Register

brand

## Users
Habitat engineers, researchers, and space-science audiences exploring combustion hazards. The confirmed primary action is launching a habitat simulation preview.

## Product Purpose
An interactive aerospace command-center landing page connecting NASA combustion research with habitat what-if scenarios and terrestrial applications. This frontend is an educational demonstrator, not a validated risk model, connected RAG service, or emergency-response system.

## Brand Personality
Technical, austere, precise. Keep the space-black design with a NASA-inspired blue/white interface: NASA Blue #0032A0 for primary actions, and lighter blue tints for readable dark-mode identity accents, highlighted data, links, controls and focus indicators. User follow-up explicitly replaces the initial red VULCAN-X/risk accents with blue as well. This supersedes the original orange/cyan branding and the intermediate blue/red palette. Natural planet surfaces and physical flame colors are independent of interface branding. Keep VULCAN-X's own identity; do not imply NASA endorsement or add NASA identifiers.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic SaaS marketing tiles, decorative browser chrome, cartoon planets, and claims of NASA certification or live AI inference.

## Design Principles
- Let the interactive orbital system be the central navigation instrument.
- Keep environment selection and telemetry synchronized.
- Distinguish research references from unvalidated demonstration outputs.
- Make the main controls work without WebGL or pointer gestures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, text labels independent of color, reduced-motion support, and responsive touch interaction. Target WCAG AA contrast and 44px touch controls.

## Reading comfort
Body copy is 16px (1rem) with 1.65 line height; controls/labels use 14px and supporting captions 12–13px minimum. Prefer sentence-case Barlow for reading and UI labels; keep Barlow Condensed for large display headings and monospace for short identifiers/numeric data. Use soft off-white primary text and brighter secondary grays on charcoal/navy cards, not pure-white glare or tiny dim text. Layouts must grow/reflow for the larger type, including expanded dossier notes; retain the approved planet geometry and NASA-blue palette.
